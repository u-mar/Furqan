## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Nadir is a calm, beautiful home for the Quran: read it, listen to it, recite it and read it together.

READ
• The Madani mushaf, page by page, in the same script as the printed copy
• Tajweed colours, or a continuous scroll you can pinch to make bigger or smaller
• Translations in more than 60 languages, with the one you choose saved for offline reading
• Bookmarks, and picking up exactly where you stopped
• Search any ayah, or find it by speaking it

LISTEN
• Renowned reciters, including Mishary Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Abdul Basit and many more
• Gapless playback that follows the ayah being recited on the page
• Download surahs and listen offline

QARI
• Record your own recitation in a quiet, focused studio
• Choose a background and post it, or keep it private
• Imitate the style of your favourite sheikh
• Swipe through recitations full screen, like and follow other reciters
• Share an ayah as a beautiful card, or post it to Qari

PRAYER
• Prayer times and the qibla direction, worked out on your phone
• The adhan, if you want it

HALAQA
• Read together with family and friends, and share a khatmah, juz by juz

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Most of Nadir works without an account
• Your reading progress, bookmarks and location stay on your phone
• No ads, no tracking networks, and nothing to buy
